Problem & Key Observation

  • Long-context LLMs are bottlenecked by the KV cache.
  • Query-agnostic context-reconstruction (KVzip) gives a reusable cache, but adds huge prefill overhead (+34 s on 124K tokens).
  • Key idea: reconstruction importance is head-dominant — no need to score all heads.
Preliminary — reconstruction importance (KVzip)
The query is the context itself (reconstruct it), not any downstream question — so scoring is query-agnostic. A KV pair's importance is the max attention it receives while reconstructing:
A = Softmax(QreconK⊤)  ·  Sl,h(i) = max g,j A[g,j,i]

HybridKV

Offline

Pre-select a fraction k of salient heads from a tiny calibration set.

Deploy

Drop pruned heads, then reconstruct-and-prune tokens only within retained heads.

Reuse

Query-agnostic: one compressed cache reused across many queries.

Results — matches full reconstruction at lower cost

Takeaway. Head-dominant reconstruction makes accurate query-agnostic KV compression cheap — pre-select heads once, prune tokens within them at deployment.
